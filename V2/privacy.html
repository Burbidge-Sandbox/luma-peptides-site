Privacy policy

Last updated September 2026 · Luma Peptides Co., 30 N Gould St, Sheridan, WY 82801

This policy describes what Luma Peptides Co. ("Luma", operating lumaresearchco.com) collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices available.

Information collected

  • Provided by you: name, organization, email, phone, and shipping address at checkout; email for waitlist or newsletter signup; the contents of contact-form messages; and your acceptance of the research-use acknowledgement and Terms, with a timestamp.
  • Collected automatically: pages viewed, products viewed and added to the cart, referring site, campaign parameters in the link you arrived from (such as utm_source), device type, browser language, screen size, and approximate timing of visits. A random identifier is stored in your browser so repeat visits can be recognized. It contains no personal details.
  • Payment: Luma does not collect or store card numbers. Payments are made through Venmo, whose own privacy policy applies to the transaction.

How it is used

  • To fulfill and ship orders, confirm payment, and respond to support requests.
  • To notify waitlist subscribers when a lot is released.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • To send marketing email if you signed up, with an unsubscribe link in every message.
  • To build advertising audiences on platforms such as Google and Meta, if those tools are enabled (see below).

Analytics and advertising tools

The site may use Google Analytics, the Google Ads tag, and the Meta Pixel. When enabled, these set cookies or similar identifiers and share browsing activity on this site with those providers, who may combine it with other information they hold. You can opt out through Google's opt-out tool, your Meta ad settings, and browser controls that block third-party cookies. Luma does not sell personal information for money.

Who it is shared with

Shipping carriers (name and address), Venmo (payment), Google (order records are stored in Google Sheets and email is sent through Google), and the analytics and advertising providers named above when enabled. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

Retention

Order records are kept for as long as needed for fulfillment, accounting, and legal obligations, generally seven years. Marketing and analytics data are kept for up to 26 months.

Accounts

Signing in uses an emailed link rather than a password. A session token is stored in your browser for 30 days and can be ended with "Sign out". Saved details (name, organization, address) are used only to prefill checkout. Email info@lumaresearchco.com to delete your account data.

Your choices and rights

You may request a copy of your personal information, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or opt out of marketing at any time by emailing info@lumaresearchco.com or writing to the address above. Requests are answered within 30 days. Residents of California and other states with privacy laws have the right to know, delete, and opt out of the sharing of personal information for targeted advertising; the same email handles those requests and Luma does not discriminate for exercising them.

Children

The site is for adults 21 and older. Luma does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21.

Changes

Updates are posted on this page with a new date.
