Shipping and returns

Shipping

  • United States only. Orders to addresses outside the U.S. are cancelled and refunded before shipment.
  • Standard (3–5 business days): $8, free on orders over $150.
  • Express (1–2 business days): $24.
  • Orders ship within one business day of lab release, in plain outer packaging with a cold pack.
  • Luma is not responsible for carrier delays, or for packages lost or stolen after a delivery scan. A signature may be required at Luma's discretion.

All sales final

Because of the nature of the products, returns are not accepted and all sales are final once an order has shipped. Orders may be cancelled for a full refund at any time before shipment.

Damaged, leaking, or incorrect items

Report damaged, leaking, missing, or incorrect items within 7 days of delivery with photographs of the item and the packaging. Verified claims are replaced or refunded at Luma's discretion. Items that have been opened are not eligible.

Chargebacks

Contact Luma before disputing a charge. A chargeback filed without first contacting support may result in the account being unable to order in future.
