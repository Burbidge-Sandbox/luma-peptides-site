Ordering

How ordering works

Luma supplies lyophilized peptides to the research community. Here is what happens between an order and a delivery.

01

Eligibility

Purchasers must be 21 or older and affiliated with a laboratory, institution, or research organization. Each order includes a research-use acknowledgement. Luma may request verification of affiliation and may decline or cancel any order.

02

Select compounds

Products are sold per vial, stated as compound and net content. Volume pricing applies automatically at three, five, and ten vials of a line. Bacteriostatic water is a separate catalog item and is not included with any peptide.

03

Lab release

Every lot is tested by an independent, accredited U.S. laboratory for purity (HPLC), identity (LC-MS), net content, and endotoxin. A lot that fails is not released. Certificates are published on the Verify a Lot page before shipping.

04

Shipping

Orders ship within one business day of lab release, within the United States only, in plain outer packaging with a cold pack. Lyophilized peptides are stable in transit at ambient temperature for several days.

05

Documentation

Each carton carries the lot number. The certificate for that lot can be looked up at any time, and full laboratory reports are available on request.

06

What Luma does not do

Luma does not provide reconstitution, handling, dosing, or usage guidance of any kind, and declines such requests. Products are not for human or animal use.

Storage and handling

Keep it cold, dry, and dark.

Lyophilized peptides are shipped as a dry powder in sealed glass vials under inert gas. Recommended storage on receipt is −20 °C, desiccated, protected from light. Under those conditions the certificate's best-before date applies.

  • Store unopened vials at −20 °C
  • Protect from light and moisture
  • Allow vials to reach room temperature before opening to avoid condensation
  • Retain the carton; the lot number is printed on it
