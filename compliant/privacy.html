Privacy policy

Last updated September 2026

This policy explains what Luma Peptides Co. collects, why, and how it is controlled.

What is collected

  • Contact, organization, and shipping details provided at checkout.
  • Order history and the research-use acknowledgement accepted with each order.
  • Basic site analytics such as pages viewed and device type.

What is not done

  • Information is never sold.
  • Full card numbers are never stored; payments are handled by a PCI-DSS-compliant processor.
  • Purchaser information is shared only with carriers and payment processors as needed to fulfill an order, or where required by law.

Choices

A copy or deletion of personal data can be requested at any time by emailing info@lumaresearchco.com.
