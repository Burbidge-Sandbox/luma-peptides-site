Frequently asked questions

Ordering

Who is eligible to order?

Purchasers must be 21 years of age or older and affiliated with a laboratory, institution, university, or research organization. Every order includes a research-use acknowledgement. Luma may request verification and may decline or cancel any order at its discretion.

Are products sold per vial?

Yes. Each listing states the compound and net content per vial. Volume pricing applies automatically at three, five, and ten vials of a line. There are no subscriptions.

Is bacteriostatic water included?

No. It is a separate catalog item under Lab Supplies and is not included with any peptide.

Quality and documentation

How is each lot tested?

By an independent, accredited U.S. laboratory for purity (HPLC), identity (LC-MS), net content, and endotoxin. Certificates are published on the Verify a Lot page before the lot ships. Full reports are available on request.

How should products be stored?

Keep it cold, dry, and dark. Unopened vials at −20 °C, desiccated and protected from light. Allow a vial to reach room temperature before opening to avoid condensation, and keep the carton, since the lot number is printed on it. Lyophilized peptides are stable in transit at ambient temperature for several days. The best-before date on the certificate applies under these conditions.

Does Luma provide handling, reconstitution, or usage guidance?

No. Luma is a chemical supplier and does not provide guidance of any kind on reconstitution, handling, or use. Requests for such guidance are declined. Products are for laboratory research use only and are not for human or animal use.

Shipping and returns

Where does Luma ship?

Within the United States only, in plain outer packaging. Orders ship within one business day of lab release.

What is the return policy?

All sales are final once an order has shipped. Items that arrive damaged, leaking, or incorrect are replaced when reported within 7 days of delivery with photographs. See Shipping and Returns.
