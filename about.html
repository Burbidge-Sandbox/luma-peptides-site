What Luma is

A chemical supplier

Luma sells reagents for in-vitro research. It is not a pharmacy, a compounding pharmacy, an outsourcing facility, or a clinic, and it does not provide usage guidance.

Independently tested

Purity by HPLC, identity by LC-MS, net content, and endotoxin, on every lot, by an accredited U.S. laboratory Luma does not own.

Published before shipping

Certificates go up on the Verify a Lot page before the first vial of a lot leaves the facility. A lot that fails is never released.

Who Luma supplies

Laboratories, institutions, and research organizations. Purchasers must be 21 or older and affiliated with such an organization, and each order includes a research-use acknowledgement. Products are for laboratory research use only and are not for human or animal use of any kind.
